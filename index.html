Mindwarp - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy is for the mobile game Mindwarp (Android package name com.mindwarp.brainpuzzle), made by the developer named on its Google Play page ("we", "us").

1. What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings and what you bought) is saved only on your phone. We have no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or location. If you uninstall the app or reset your progress, this data is deleted.

2. Ads

Mindwarp is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your phone's advertising ID, your IP address (which can show your approximate location), device information and how you use the app, to show ads, measure them and stop fraud. You can choose "personalised" or "non-personalised" ads inside the app (Settings > Privacy & ads). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases

All payments are handled by Google Play. We only learn that a purchase was made (which item and an order number). We never see your card, bank or payment details.

4. Reminders (notifications)

If you allow it, Mindwarp sends reminders to come back and play. They are created and scheduled on your phone. No data about you is sent anywhere for this. You can turn reminders off in the app (Settings) or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Children

Mindwarp is made for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will help.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to see, correct or delete your data and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of data for ads. We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers. Inside the app you can choose non-personalised ads and turn on "Do not sell or share my info". For ad data held by Google, use your Google account privacy controls or your phone's ad settings.

7. Security

Network traffic for ads and purchases uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

9. Contact

Questions or requests? Write to the developer email shown on Mindwarp's Google Play store page.
